Enter Your Monthly Income

Enter your monthly take-home pay (after taxes). The 50/30/20 rule will divide it into recommended spending categories.

Your 50/30/20 Budget

Based on a monthly income of $0

50% — Needs Rent, groceries, utilities, transportation, minimum debt payments
$0
30% — Wants Dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, shopping, hobbies
$0
20% — Savings & Debt Payoff Emergency fund, retirement contributions, extra debt payments
